# Leave Application & Time-Off Process

## Types of Leave
QuickDesk provides several categories of paid time off (PTO):
1. **Annual Paid Time Off**: Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days per month (21 days annually).
2. **Sick & Wellness Leave**: 10 days per calendar year. No medical certificate is required for absences under 2 consecutive days. For 3 or more consecutive sick days, submit a physician note.
3. **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks fully paid leave for primary caregivers; 6 weeks for secondary caregivers.

## How to Submit a Leave Request
- Log in to BambooHR at `https://hr.company.internal/timeoff`.
- Select your leave category, start date, and end date.
- Requests of 3 days or longer must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Your direct manager receives an automated approval notification. Once approved, your Slack status updates automatically to 'Out of Office'.

## Unused Leave Rollover
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can roll over to the following calendar year. Any remaining unused balance beyond 5 days is paid out in December payroll.
