# Expense Reimbursement & Stipend Policy

## Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for business-related expenses incurred on behalf of the company:
- **Home Office Setup**: One-time allowance up to $500 for ergonomic chairs, desks, monitors, and webcams upon joining.
- **Monthly Internet & Mobile Allowance**: Up to $75 per month for remote workers.
- **Client Meals & Travel**: Flight tickets (economy class), lodging up to $200/night, and meals up to $60/day during pre-approved client travel.

## Submission Guidelines
1. Submit expense reports via Expensify (`https://expenses.company.internal`).
2. Itemized receipts are mandatory for every transaction above $25. Credit card statement summaries are not accepted by accounting.
3. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
4. Approved expense claims submitted before the 20th of the month are disbursed with the current month's payroll direct deposit.
