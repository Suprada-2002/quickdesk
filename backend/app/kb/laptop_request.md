# Hardware Refresh & Laptop Request Policy

## Hardware Standards
QuickDesk engineers and staff receive standard-issue hardware based on role:
- **Engineering / Data**: 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro (M3 Pro, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD) or Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 6 (Intel i7, 32GB RAM, Ubuntu/Windows).
- **Operations / Product / HR**: 14-inch Apple MacBook Air (M3, 16GB RAM) or Dell XPS 13.

## Refresh Cycle & Battery Replacements
- Standard laptop hardware is scheduled for replacement every 24 to 36 months.
- If your laptop battery degraded below 60% health or holds less than 1 hour of charge, open an IT ticket with a battery diagnostic screenshot for an immediate free battery replacement.
- Broken or damaged hardware must be reported immediately. Accidental damage protection covers one repair per 12-month period.

## Requesting New Hardware
To request a new laptop, docking station, or secondary 4K monitor, submit a request ticket with manager sign-off. Processing takes 3 to 5 business days for standard shipping.
