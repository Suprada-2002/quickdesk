# Finance Shared Drive Access Policy

## Security Classification
All documents on the Finance Shared Drive (Google Workspace Shared Drive 'Finance-Confidential') are classified as Highly Confidential. This includes payroll ledgers, budget spreadsheets, executive compensation models, and quarterly tax filings.

## Access Levels
- **Read-Only**: Available to department heads and project leads needing quarterly budget visibility.
- **Read-Write / Content Manager**: Restricted strictly to active members of the Corporate Finance, Accounting, and Tax teams.

## Access Request Procedure
1. Standard employees do not receive direct access to raw financial drives.
2. If your project requires financial data, submit a ticket specifying the exact Google Sheet or folder path, business justification, and duration needed.
3. Requests require explicit written approval from the Head of Finance or Chief Financial Officer.
4. Access grants automatically expire after 90 days unless an annual extension is formally approved.
